## What I Built

**Trekkie is an offline trek-planning agent that refuses to guess.**

I built it for a friend who plans and executes his treks in a self-supported way. His trek information can easily end up scattered across messages, spreadsheets, screenshots, and notes, making it easy to miss important details.

Trekkie helps bring everything into one place and checks the plan for:

- Missing information
- Contradictions
- Unconfirmed details
- Safety-critical unknowns
- Dependencies between different parts of the trek

The agent asks **one question at a time**, prioritizing the things that matter most.

Every piece of information can have a status such as:

**Confirmed · User-provided · Assumed · Unknown · Conflicting · Outdated**

The app never turns an assumption into a fact.

## What It Does

The trekker can enter:

- Trek dates and stages
- Route and transport
- Accommodation and camps
- Food and water
- Gear
- Hazards
- Emergency contacts
- Other important trek details

Trekkie then helps identify what is still missing or needs verification.

When the plan is ready, it provides a simple day-by-day execution view with route information, water, camps, hazards, and important decision points.
